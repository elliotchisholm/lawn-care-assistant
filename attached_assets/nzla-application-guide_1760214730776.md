# NZLA Lawn Application Guide - Weekly Schedule

**All rates shown are per 100m²**

## Important Notes
- Apply products minimum 6 hours after mowing
- Apply when temperatures do not exceed 25°C
- Avoid mowing for 24 hours after application
- If irrigation is not specified, products are foliar feeds and left on the plant
- Apply to dry foliage
- All tank mixes are in 5L of water per 100m²

---

## January - Week 1 (ISO Week 1)

**Day 1:**
- NZLA Wetter or Wetter 3W: 250ml
- Follow with 15-20mm irrigation

**Once foliage is dry:**
- Root Health: 50ml
- Iron+: 200ml;
- Nurture: 400ml
- Humic+: 50ml
